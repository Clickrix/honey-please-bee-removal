About Honey Please

Honey Please Bee Removal is run by beekeeper Karen Palmer. Honeybee removal combines beekeeping experience with construction expertise — locating the colony with inspection cameras, removing it with a special bee vacuum, then sealing the space so bees can’t return. Every rescued colony is given a new home.
